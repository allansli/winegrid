Climate and colour only. A row marked **applied** is on the card. A row marked **proposed, not applied** is a sibling gap that this pass did not copy. Soil, UNESCO, thin-skin, single-varietal, oak, and family tags stay as the Sommelier wrote them.

Siblings: Côte de Beaune and Côte de Nuits reds against Gevrey-Chambertin; Burgundy whites against Meursault; Loire against Sancerre (and Muscadet for Melon); Rueda against Rioja's white half; grapes against Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Sauvignon Blanc, Viognier, Albariño, or Chenin.
